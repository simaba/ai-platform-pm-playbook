# Build vs Buy Framework for AI Platforms

This framework supports a structured product decision. It does not replace procurement, security, legal, privacy, architecture, or financial approval.

## Step 2: Gate before scoring

Eliminate any option that cannot satisfy a non-negotiable requirement, for example:

- required data residency or confidentiality boundary
- legal or regulatory requirement
- safety, security, privacy, or accessibility constraint
- unacceptable vendor lock-in or continuity risk
- inability to meet a fixed launch, reliability, or integration requirement

A weighted score should not compensate for a failed non-negotiable gate.

## Step 3: Compare viable options

| Dimension | Questions | Suggested evidence |
|---|---|---|
| User and product fit | Does it support the required journey and differentiation? | prototype results, user research, acceptance criteria |
| Time to value | Can it meet the required milestone with realistic dependencies? | delivery plan, integration estimate |
| Total cost of ownership | What are build, licensing, operations, migration, and exit costs? | cost model with assumptions |
| Technical fit | Does it integrate with the target architecture and observability model? | architecture review, proof of concept |
| Risk and control | Can the organization govern security, privacy, safety, and reliability? | risk review, control mapping |
| Vendor viability | What is the support, roadmap, portability, and exit position? | due diligence, contract review |
| Operating model | Who owns incidents, changes, evaluation, and lifecycle work? | RACI, service model |

## Step 4: Record assumptions

For each score or narrative conclusion, identify:

- evidence source
- confidence level
- unresolved dependency
- accountable owner
- review date

## Step 5: Make a reversible decision where possible

Prefer a bounded pilot, abstraction layer, contract exit clause, or staged migration when uncertainty is material. Define the decision trigger for expanding, changing, or stopping the chosen path.
